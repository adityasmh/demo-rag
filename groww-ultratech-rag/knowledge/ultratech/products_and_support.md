# UltraTech products, tools and support (public information, read on 2026-10-22)

## Other building products
Apart from cement, UltraTech sells ready mix concrete for infrastructure, waterproofing systems (including Weather Pro), tile fixing products (TILEFIXO), crack filler, epoxy tile grout (Style Epoxy Grout) and Readiplast Pro, a plaster system. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/

## Planning tools
The UltraTech website has free tools for home builders: a cost calculator, a store locator, a product predictor, an EMI calculator and a tile calculator. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/homebuilding-explained/home-planning-tools/cost-calculator

## Contact and dealership
Customers can call the toll free number 1800 210 3311. The Get In Touch form on the website is used for product purchase questions and for dealership or retailership requests. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/

## Fraud warning
UltraTech says it does not sell products through SMS, WhatsApp, phone calls, emails or social media, and that it never asks for advance payment. Suspicious activity can be reported at cybercrime.gov.in or on the helpline 1930. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/
