# UltraTech cement types (public information, read on 2026-10-22)

## OPC grades
OPC means Ordinary Portland Cement. UltraTech lists it in grades 33, 43, 53 and 53-S, and the grade number comes from the strength of the cement after 28 days. Grade 33 is for general use. Grade 43 is mainly for normal concrete and masonry. Grade 53 is for high strength structural work such as prestressed concrete. Grade 53-S is a special grade for railway sleepers. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/products/overview/opc

## PPC
PPC means Portland Pozzolana Cement. It is OPC mixed with fly ash (15 to 35 percent). It is used for most jobs where OPC is used, except where very high early strength is needed, and it resists moisture and sulphates well, which helps in marine and hydraulic work. UltraTech says PPC has no grade number. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/products/overview/ppc

## OPC versus PPC
UltraTech publishes a separate article comparing OPC and PPC for home builders, which is the place to read the detailed differences. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/home-building-explained-single/descriptive-articles/difference-between-opc-and-ppc-cement

## UltraTech Super
UltraTech Super is a blended cement that UltraTech describes as a modified PPC. It is meant for fast construction and has a lower carbon footprint than OPC 53. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/products/overview/ultratech-super

## UltraTech Premium
UltraTech Premium is a blend that uses silica and slag. It is positioned for plain concrete, masonry, RCC and places with sulphate or marine exposure. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/products/overview/ultratech-premium

## UltraTech Weather Plus
Weather Plus is marketed as protection against dampness for homes. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/products/ultratech-building-solution/ultratech-weather-plus

## Birla Shakti
Birla Shakti is another cement brand sold by UltraTech, with OPC 53, OPC 43 and PPC options. Source: https://www.ultratechcement.com/for-homebuilders/products/birla-shakti
