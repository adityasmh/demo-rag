# Groww charges (public information, read on 2026-10-22)

## Account opening and maintenance
Opening a trading and demat account on Groww costs nothing, and the account maintenance charge is also listed as zero. Source: https://groww.in/pricing and https://groww.in/help/stocks,-f&o,-ipo-&-mtf/sx-pricing/what-are-account-opening-charges

## Equity brokerage
For stocks Groww charges the lower of Rs 20 or 0.1 percent of the trade value on each executed order, with a minimum of Rs 5. GST of 18 percent is added on brokerage. Note: one older Groww help page quotes 0.05 percent instead of 0.1 percent, so the pricing page should be treated as the main source and checked before relying on it. Source: https://groww.in/pricing

## F&O brokerage
For futures and options Groww charges a flat Rs 20 per executed order. Source: https://groww.in/pricing

## Charges when selling shares
Selling shares from the demat account has a depository participant (DP) charge. Groww lists its own part as Rs 16.5 per debit and says it is not charged when the debit value is under Rs 100. The depository adds a small charge of about Rs 3.25 to Rs 3.5, and 18 percent GST applies. Source: https://groww.in/help/stocks,-f&o,-ipo-&-mtf/sx-demat-account/how-much-does-groww-charge-for-stocks-1

## Direct mutual funds
Groww says direct mutual funds can be bought through lump sum or SIP with zero charges from Groww. Source: https://groww.in/pricing

## Where to see the full list of charges
Groww has a help article explaining the different charges that appear in the trade report. Source: https://groww.in/help/stocks,-f&o,-ipo-&-mtf/discoverable/what-are-the-different-charges-mentioned-in-the-report
